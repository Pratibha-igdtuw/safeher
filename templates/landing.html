How it works

From a normal walk to a rescued moment

The same guardian loop runs quietly in the background, every time.

Your journey starts

Open SafeHer and your session begins — no setup, no delay.

Location tracking activates

Your Bubble — the people you've chosen — can see your live location if you turn sharing on.

AI monitors the surroundings

Voice-distress detection listens for panic patterns; route scoring watches the path ahead.

An unsafe situation is flagged

A scream is detected, a check-in timer lapses, or you hit SOS yourself.

Trusted contacts are notified

Your Bubble gets your live location and an alert immediately.

Nearby help is one tap away

Safety Map

See a route's safety score before you walk it

Tap any spot on the map for a six-point safety audit — lighting, openness, walkpath condition, security presence, transport access and crowd density — colour-coded from green to red.

📞

Fake Call

Exit an uncomfortable moment, convincingly

Schedule a realistic incoming call for now, or a few seconds from now — a quiet way out when you need one.

🎙️

Voice ML Monitoring

Real audio classification, not a keyword filter

A spectrogram is computed on your device and classified server-side for scream and alarm sounds — the raw audio itself never leaves your phone.

👭

Bubble & Community

A circle that can see you, and a feed that watches your area

Invite trusted contacts to your Bubble for live tracking, and post or browse real-time alerts and safe-spot tips from your community.

Account Security
